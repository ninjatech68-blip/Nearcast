# Nearcast Legal And Community Policy Outline

## Document Control

- **Status:** Product-policy baseline requiring legal review before public beta
- **Last updated:** 2026-08-24
- **Governed by:** [Nearcast Documentation Reference](./00 - Start Here - Nearcast Project Reference.md)

## Important Notice

This document defines product requirements and policy structure. It is not legal advice and is not a substitute for qualified legal counsel. Public launch requires jurisdiction-specific review, especially for privacy, intermediary liability, consumer protection, location data, minors, and messaging regulations.

## Community Principles

- Express genuine intents.
- Describe material terms accurately.
- Respect reach and privacy boundaries.
- Respond only when relevant.
- Follow through on accepted commitments or communicate promptly.
- Do not harass, pressure, discriminate against, or endanger others.
- Use block and report tools when an interaction feels unsafe.

## Prohibited Uses

- Illegal goods, controlled substances, or weapons.
- Sexual services, exploitation, trafficking, or non-consensual content.
- Unsafe contact with minors or attempts to bypass guardian protections.
- Hate, threats, harassment, stalking, or doxxing.
- Fraud, impersonation, counterfeit goods, and deceptive schemes.
- Requests for passwords, OTPs, financial credentials, or identity documents.
- Sale or disclosure of private personal data.
- Malware, phishing, spam, or platform manipulation.
- Dangerous medical claims or emergency-service impersonation.
- Fabricated confirmations, outcomes, or trust evidence.

## Restricted Uses

The following require additional controls before broad support:

- Housing and flatmate matching.
- Employment and paid services.
- High-value goods.
- Financial requests or fundraising.
- Health-related recommendations.
- Private-home meetings.
- Intents involving vulnerable people.

Restricted uses may receive narrower reach, additional verification, warnings, manual review, or complete MVP exclusion.

## Terms Of Service Requirements

The final Terms must cover:

- Account eligibility and minimum age.
- Accurate information and account security.
- License to display user-submitted content within approved reach.
- User control over intent reach and expiry.
- Prohibited and restricted uses.
- No guarantee of identity, safety, availability, or successful outcome.
- User responsibility for offline interactions.
- Moderation, suspension, and appeal rights.
- Intellectual property and infringement reporting.
- Service availability and limitation of liability.
- Governing law and dispute process.
- Account termination and data handling.

## Privacy Policy Requirements

The final Privacy Policy must disclose:

- Data collected during authentication and profile creation.
- Intent, response, message, location, verification, device, and interaction data.
- Data generated through matching, trust context, moderation, and analytics.
- Purpose and legal basis for processing.
- Service providers and subprocessors.
- How controlled reach and progressive disclosure work.
- Retention periods.
- User rights to access, correct, export, and delete data.
- International data transfers where applicable.
- Security practices without making absolute guarantees.
- Contact route for privacy requests.

## Location Policy

- Explain approximate versus exact location.
- Collect location only with permission and product need.
- Do not sell precise location data.
- Do not include exact location in advertising or analytics profiles.
- Let users revoke location permission and edit stored area.
- Delete exact coordination location according to the retention schedule.

## Moderation And Enforcement

Possible actions:

- Remove intent or message.
- Reduce reach.
- Warn user.
- Require verification.
- Temporarily restrict features.
- Suspend account.
- Permanently remove account.
- Preserve and disclose evidence when legally required.

Actions should be proportionate to severity, recurrence, credibility, and immediate risk. Severe harm may justify immediate restriction before full review.

## Appeals

- Provide an in-product or email appeal route.
- Require the affected action identifier and factual explanation.
- Separate appeal review from the original decision when staffing allows.
- Do not disclose reporter identity or sensitive moderation evidence.
- Record appeal outcome and rationale.

## Law-Enforcement And Emergency Requests

- Publish a valid request channel before public beta.
- Verify requester identity and legal authority.
- Disclose only legally required data.
- Preserve an audit trail.
- Notify users unless prohibited or unsafe.
- Clearly state that Nearcast is not an emergency service.

## Intellectual Property

- Users retain ownership of their content.
- Nearcast receives a limited license necessary to distribute content within selected reach.
- Provide a route for copyright and trademark complaints.
- Remove repeat infringers when legally required.

## Policy UX Requirements

- Summarize key rules during onboarding without requiring users to parse full legal text.
- Show contextual rules before elevated-risk actions.
- Explain material policy changes and obtain consent where required.
- Keep Terms, Privacy Policy, Community Rules, and support contact accessible in the app.
- Provide account deletion from the app rather than email-only deletion.

## Pre-Public-Beta Legal Checklist

- Qualified counsel reviews Terms and Privacy Policy.
- Data-flow and subprocessor inventory is complete.
- Age eligibility and minors controls are implemented.
- India-specific privacy and messaging obligations are reviewed.
- App Store and Google Play privacy declarations match actual behavior.
- Account deletion works end to end.
- Moderation and law-enforcement request processes are operational.
