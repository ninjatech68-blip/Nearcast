Nearcast · Screen Mockups v2 · rev after review

The whole app on one page

Every MVP screen at 320 × 690 (iPhone target), in the app's own tokens Manrope, cream ground, forest-green primary. This is v2 after the founder's review: real SVG icons in the tab bar (not text glyphs), proper iOS navigation headers on every screen with cancel/back and primary actions, one CTA per feed card with "Why shown" as a chip, safety in a menu, and delete as a two-step confirmation, not a red button under Sign out. Toggle your OS to see dark.
