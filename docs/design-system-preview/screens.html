Nearcast · Screen Mockups v3 · 5-tab IA · native gestures

The whole app on one page

Every MVP screen at 320 × 690 iPhone target, in the app's own tokens. This is v3 after the founder's UI/UX review. Five tabs: For You · Explore · Broadcast (opens a modal compose sheet — not a destination) · Chat · Profile, matching the design board and the Threads pattern. iOS-native chrome throughout: three-column nav bars with back chevron and primary action, sheets with detents, long-press context menus, swipe-to-action rows, blur tab bar over content. Both light and dark palettes are defined.
