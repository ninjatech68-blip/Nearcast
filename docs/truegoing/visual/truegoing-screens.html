Truegoing screens

Fourteen screens of the first version in the Airbnb direction ruled on 2026-09-24, lightened on the owner's call: four tabs, three-line cards, the why line only when the reason is not obvious, no monospace. Icon positions are neutral slots until the 3D set arrives (3dicons.co, public domain, or commissioned), so the layout is judged and not a placeholder: Airbnb's colour roles with Truegoing coral in the brand slot, white surfaces, soft shadows, a category row of three-dimensional plan icons, a full-screen filter sheet with a live count, illustrated empty states. Every word on these screens is a proposal you can strike. Toggle dark to check both looks, and switch the palette to compare the three accent options on the same screens.

Names, plans and numbers are examples. Icons stand in for the final drawn set.

5 · Colour

Ruled 2026-09-24: Airbnb's colour roles, Truegoing's hue. Text near-black #222222, muted #717171, lines #DDDDDD, surfaces white, brand coral #FF5C39 on the gradient primary button and the tick, black for secondary actions and selected states. The coral is deliberately not Airbnb's #FF385C: same role, our hue, so the set of layout, icon style and colour is not theirs. The older options stay in the switch for comparison.

OptionWhat it saysStrengthRisk
Orange #FF4D1DEnergy, go, warmthRecognisable from the old app; vibrantReads as food delivery in India (Swiggy, Zomato); collides with the Social category colour; loud on every button
Green #17442ETrust, settled, showed upThe promise as a colour; few rivals in this category; calmWhatsApp adjacency at a glance; can feel dull in marketing
Ink #15130FQuiet app, loud plansCategory colours are the only colour, so the plan is the headline; timeless; Uber and Hinge prove itFeels cold if icons and photos are weak; less "vibrant" at first sight

6 · Icon set

Ruled 2026-09-24: the plan icons are three-dimensional rendered objects in Airbnb's style. Nothing drawn here can match a render, so the screens above carry neutral slots instead of an imitation. Two legal ways to fill them, either of which drops in without a screen change:

RouteWhat it isCost and time
3dicons.co (recommended to start)Over a thousand Blender-rendered objects released into the public domain (CC0): sports gear, food, music, games, tools, travel, in several colour styles. The closest free match to Airbnb's toy-like look. Download the set, pick one style, and I map objects to our 56 sub-interests and categories, commissioning only the handful that are missing.Free. One afternoon to map.
CommissionA 3D illustrator renders the 56 to the brief in document 14 §4: one stage, one light, one material, Airbnb's 2025 set as the reference. Yields a set that is ours alone.Roughly two to four weeks.
Not an optionCopying Airbnb's own icon files. Their copyright; an App Store takedown waiting to happen.

Flat outline glyphs for navigation and marks remain in visual/icons/; those are ours and stay.
