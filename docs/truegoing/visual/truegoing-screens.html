Truegoing screens

Twelve screens of the first version, drawn to the direction in document 14 with the copy rewritten so that a first-time user needs no explanation. Every word on these screens is a proposal you can strike. Toggle dark to check both looks, and switch the palette to compare the three accent options on the same screens.

Names, plans and numbers are examples. Icons stand in for the final drawn set.

5 · Colour

Three candidates for the accent, on the same screens above. Six category colours carry the plans in all three. Backgrounds are the system's, so dark mode is free and correct.

OptionWhat it saysStrengthRisk
Orange #FF4D1DEnergy, go, warmthRecognisable from the old app; vibrantReads as food delivery in India (Swiggy, Zomato); collides with the Social category colour; loud on every button
Green #17442ETrust, settled, showed upThe promise as a colour; few rivals in this category; calmWhatsApp adjacency at a glance; can feel dull in marketing

6 · Icon set

Eighty-five drawn icons in one style: 24-point grid, 1.8 stroke, round ends, one shape filled at 18% for a second tone. Category icons are the fallback when a plan's words match nothing. Marks and navigation use the same hand so the app has one voice in pictures as well as words. Files are in visual/icons/.
