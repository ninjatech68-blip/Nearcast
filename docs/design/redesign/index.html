nearcast v2 · design system + screens · function unchanged from v1

Cast it.

The v1 skin read enterprise: sage panels, banner stacks, five tabs, polite copy. All of it is gone. A cast is now a poster that fills the screen, color does the categorizing, and the app talks like a person with somewhere to be. Trust stays visible because that is the product.

00

The call

Primary: Gen Z, 17 to 27. Nearcast moves real-world plans through vouched trust chains, which is adult social-graph behavior; a meetup app should not court under-16s. Gen Alpha still gets the parts that transfer: gesture nav, system lines under ten words, motion doing the explaining. Nothing is averaged between the two.

  • Posters, not cardsOne cast fills the viewport. The feed is a vertical snap through flat color blocks, TikTok's chrome discipline without its scroll trap: casts expire, the feed ends.
  • Color is the categoryTen categories, ten flat fields: social orange, sports yellow, food salmon, music ink, travel green, games cobalt, arts lavender, learning sky, networking slate, help cream. One category per cast.
  • The name always shows tooThe category renders in type above the headline, so color is never the only carrier and the poster reads for colorblind users. The ask ("need two", "face value") lives in the headline words, where people write it anyway.
  • Chrome never changesInk and orange stay the only chrome colors. Buttons, the rail, and sheets look identical on every category; only the field moves. Pills follow the opposite-pole rule so they always read as controls.
  • Three nav pointsFeed, cast, activity. Profile lives behind the avatar dot. Five tabs was two too many.
  • Trust stays loudVouch count and the "why you" line ride on every poster. Privacy disclosures are one mono line, not a banner. This is the non-negotiable product rule, restyled, never removed.
  • No pressure mechanicsExpiry is a fact ("gone 10pm"), never a countdown. No streaks, no guilt copy, nothing that works by making you feel bad for leaving.
  • Reputation is receiptsNobody rates anybody. Signal is built from confirmed attendance, and the reward is range (who your next cast reaches), not rank. Section 05.

03

Seven primitives

Poster, bar, sheet, row, tag, field, bars. Every screen below is built from these and nothing else; each one appears in at least two screens. If a screen seems to need an eighth, the screen is wrong.

  • posterfull-bleed category-colored block: category tag, cast headline, tappable caster pill, meta line, why line, bar. used: feed, cast-sent, recap, empty, detail underlay.
  • barone 58px button plus at most one quiet text action. used: every screen with an action.
  • sheetbottom sheet for everything secondary: detail, reach, request note. progressive disclosure lives here, not in chrome.
  • rowmono-subtitled list line. used: activity, your casts, profile.
  • tagmono caps chip for categories and states (new / matched / draft). used: posters, rows, compose, filter, onboarding.
  • fielddisplay-size text input, orange caret, no border. typing looks like the poster it becomes. used: compose, request note.
  • barsthe one data visual: five signal bars, filled by attendance facts only. never a number in public. used: detail sheet, profile, recap.

05

Signal

The reason to come back when you have no plan to make. One reputation object with three faces, and every input is an attendance fact, so "strong signal" can only ever mean "shows up", never "charming".

  • receipts · publica plan both sides confirm happened becomes a permanent log line: "31 plans made real · last: badminton, tuesday". facts, not judgment. nobody rates anybody, ever.
  • range · privatestrong signal widens who your next cast actually reaches; flaking on a confirmed plan narrows it for a while. reputation pays out as utility, not vanity. only you see the number.
  • recap · monthlya shareable poster in the verb colors. identity expression, zero pressure, and the organic-growth surface off-platform.
  • guardrailsno rating UI exists. absence never decays anything. nothing public is a comparable number: public is a log, private is a reach. new users start mid-signal, never at zero.

07

Motion & micro-interactions

Three curves, used everywhere, never per-screen. State changes move like objects: posters wipe, sheets slide with weight, confirmations stamp with overshoot. Nothing crossfades, nothing loops except the loader, and no animation blocks input.

  • --press · 120mstiming, cubic-bezier(.3,.7,.4,1). every tap scales to .96 on touch-down. input is never silent.
  • --snap · 220msovershoot arrival. reanimated spring: damping 14 · stiffness 280 · mass 0.9. stamps, tags, bars, radio picks.
  • --slide · 300msdecisive move, no bounce. reanimated spring: damping 22 · stiffness 220 · mass 1. sheets, strips, wipes.

08

Navigation

No tab bar exists. The root is a two-page horizontal pager (feed ↔ activity) with one rail floating over both; everything else is a native iOS presentation. The whole model fits in six sentences:

  • up / downnext or previous cast. vertical is always content.
  • left / rightfeed ↔ activity. horizontal is always pages, so no row or poster ever owns a horizontal swipe.
  • swipe up on text, or tapopens that thing's sheet (cast detail, activity row). drag down closes it.
  • the + on the railcompose, as a full-screen modal, from anywhere.
  • the avatar dotyou, as a sheet. drag down to leave.
  • the railnear · + · activity. mirrors every gesture as a tap, fades while you scroll, returns when you settle. buttons are the fallback, never the only way.

Platform

The rules that make it feel native instead of themed. Expo SDK 57, React Native, iOS-first.

concernrule
safe areasposters and modals draw edge-to-edge and pad content by inset; the wordmark row sits at top inset + 4; floating cast button at bottom inset + 8. nothing hugs the notch or the home indicator
