nearcast v2 · design system + screens · function unchanged from v1

Cast it.

The v1 skin read enterprise: sage panels, banner stacks, five tabs, polite copy. All of it is gone. A cast is now a poster that fills the screen, color does the categorizing, and the app talks like a person with somewhere to be. Trust stays visible because that is the product.

00

The call

Primary: Gen Z, 17 to 27. Nearcast moves real-world plans through vouched trust chains, which is adult social-graph behavior; a meetup app should not court under-16s. Gen Alpha still gets the parts that transfer: gesture nav, system lines under ten words, motion doing the explaining. Nothing is averaged between the two.

  • Posters, not cardsOne cast fills the viewport. The feed is a vertical snap through flat color blocks, TikTok's chrome discipline without its scroll trap: casts expire, the feed ends.
  • Color is the categoryNEED is yellow, GOT is green, LET'S is orange. The verb chip from v1 is deleted because the background already says it.
  • Verbs start sentencesNEED / GOT / LET'S read as the first word of what the person typed, so every headline sounds like someone talking.
  • Three nav pointsFeed, cast, activity. Profile lives behind the avatar dot. Five tabs was two too many.
  • Trust stays loudVouch count and the "why you" line ride on every poster. Privacy disclosures are one mono line, not a banner. This is the non-negotiable product rule, restyled, never removed.
  • No pressure mechanicsExpiry is a fact ("gone 10pm"), never a countdown. No streaks, no guilt copy, nothing that works by making you feel bad for leaving.
01

Color

Five values total. Ink and cream are the neutrals. Orange dominates because casting is sending up a flare. Yellow and green exist only as the other two verb fields. There are no gradients, no tints, no elevation shadows on surfaces.

Type

Two families. Bricolage Grotesque carries everything a person says: its warped grotesque shapes read flyer, not SaaS. IBM Plex Mono carries everything the system knows: place, vouches, expiry, reasons. That split is the whole hierarchy; there are no dividers or card borders anywhere in the app.

03

Six primitives

Poster, bar, sheet, row, tag, field. Every screen below is built from these and nothing else; each one appears in at least two screens. If a screen seems to need a seventh, the screen is wrong.

  • posterfull-bleed verb-colored block: tag, cast headline, meta line, why line, bar. used: feed, cast-sent, empty, detail underlay.
  • barone 58px button plus at most one quiet text action. used: every screen with an action.
  • sheetbottom sheet for everything secondary: detail, reach, request note. progressive disclosure lives here, not in chrome.
  • rowmono-subtitled list line. used: activity, your casts, profile.
  • tagmono caps chip for verbs and states (new / matched / draft). used: posters, rows, compose.
  • fielddisplay-size text input, orange caret, no border. typing looks like the poster it becomes. used: compose, request note.

Second person, present tense, everywhere. Buttons say what the user says ("I'm in", "cast it", "send it"). System lines are mono, lowercase, under ten words, and always name the next move. No slang, no exclamation marks, no apologizing.

06

Motion

Three tokens, used everywhere, never per-screen. State changes move like objects: posters wipe in as color blocks, sheets slide with weight, confirmations stamp with overshoot. Nothing crossfades.

  • --press · 120msevery tap scales to .96 immediately. input is never silent.
  • --snap · 220msovershoot curve for arrivals: stamps, tags, the "in" confirmation.
  • --slide · 300mssheets and poster wipes. one decisive move, no bounce.
